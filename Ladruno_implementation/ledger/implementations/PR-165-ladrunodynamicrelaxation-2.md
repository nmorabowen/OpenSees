| **LadrunoDynamicRelaxation** — quasi-static solver by **explicit, matrix-free dynamic relaxation** (the explicit sibling of LadrunoArcLength): fictitious-mass leap-frog + **Cundall kinetic damping** driven to static rest, under the stock `DirectIntegrationAnalysis` driver with `algorithm Linear` + `system Diagonal` (NO analysis-core surgery). **`M*` = integrator-owned Gershgorin row-sum fictitious mass** (`m_i=(dt²/4)Σ_j|K_ij|`, scale-free; built once in `domainChanged` via an SOE-free element-stiffness probe), assembled onto the diagonal SOE by an overridden `formTangent()` so the solve degenerates to a `M*⁻¹` apply — deliberately NOT `getMass()` (zero on zero-density models, the ADR-20 BLOCKER). Modes `-mass {gershgorin\|lumped\|unity}`. True static residual `‖f_ext−f_int‖=‖M*·a‖` (no `f_v` pollution). Termination is **script-owned** via `Ladruno_scripts/relax_to_static.py` (velocity+displacement decay; the stock transient driver has no convergence early-exit). Differentiates from `ExplicitDifferenceStatic` (33001, user-tuned Rayleigh) by auto-tuning everything — the LS-DYNA `*CONTROL_DYNAMIC_RELAXATION` sibling Abaqus lacks. ~70% leap-frog reuse from CentralDifferenceLadruno. **v2 (same PR): `-damping viscous` (viscous-critical mode, lagged-velocity, `cVisc=4ζs/dt` with `s=√(1+ζ²)−ζ` so the explicit damping stays stable) + auto-refresh (`M*` rebuilt at each kinetic-energy peak where velocities are already zero ⇒ snap-through is stable with NO manual `-recompute`).** **v3 (#728) — `-massSafety $f`, a SILENT-WRONG-ANSWER fix.** The v1 Gershgorin mass attained its own bound with EQUALITY (`ω_max·dt ≤ 2`, the central-difference limit itself), i.e. it marched EXACTLY ON the stability boundary with no margin, where the amplification matrix is defective and round-off is amplified rather than damped — and DR takes orders of magnitude more steps than a physical explicit run, so it sat there long enough for the growth to become the answer. Measured (note 83 §3): a zero-push hold started from an EXACT equilibrium walked to an **87 kN residual on a 300 kN problem**, reporting no error, and on an elastic-plastic model the softening it manufactured was **permanent**. `-massSafety f` divides the prefactor by `f²` (`m_i=(dt²/(4f²))Σ_j|K_ij|` ⇒ `ω_max·dt ≤ 2f`); it is algebraically identical to the old script-side workaround (size for `-dt D`, march at `f·D` — both give `du=4f²R/Σ|K_ij|`) but discoverable from a deck, and it scales `cVisc=4ζsf/dt` with it. **The DEFAULT is 0.5, NOT 1, so results change for existing decks:** relaxation per step scales as `f²`, so every DR run now relaxes 4× less per step; residual-gated decks reach the same answer for ~4× the steps, step-count-gated decks silently under-relax and must have budgets raised, and `-massSafety 1` restores the old behaviour with a warning naming what was opted into. **0.5 is justified on the measured thresholds, not chosen:** the safe `f` is element- and state-dependent (H8 bbar fails 0.85 / holds 0.75; H20 uri fails 0.75, holds 0.50 elastic, needs **0.25 deeply plastic**), so 0.5 is the largest value under every measured ELASTIC threshold at a 4× cost, while 0.25 would impose 16× on every run for a model-specific case that is now DETECTED instead. **Detector:** `ladrunoDR stabilityMargin` = `(ω_max·dt/2)²` of the mass actually in use, measured against the LIVE tangent — exactly `f²` on an unchanged tangent, `>1` once the model has stiffened past its mass (the deep-plastic mechanism) — with a one-time WARNING on crossing, paired with a parse-time warning for `-massSafety ≥ 1`, which sits at exactly 1.0 forever and so cannot be caught by a `>1` test. Sampled BOTH free at every `M*` rebuild (dense, timed at KE peaks) AND by an independent probe every `-marginEvery N` steps (default 500, `0` = off): measuring only at a rebuild made the diagnostic blind under `-noAutoRefresh` (1.07 vs a flat 1.00 on the same diverging run) and a fixed cadence alone is too coarse for a transient excursion. Reports the WORST since `domainChanged`, so a refresh cannot erase the evidence of the excursion it papers over; NEGATIVE = not measured (−1 no gershgorin mass, −2 probe off) and never means safe. **Validated as an equality:** scaling `E` by a known `r` gives margin `f²·r` to the last digit (r = 1.5/2/4/6/8 → 0.375/0.500/1.000/1.500/2.000, refresh policy immaterial), crossing 1 exactly at `r = 1/f² = 4` — `f = 0.5` buys precisely 4× tangent headroom. `-massSafety` is gershgorin-only (lumped/unity carry no Gershgorin bound; ignored + warned, margin reports −1). Deferred v4: per-region KE zeroing.
