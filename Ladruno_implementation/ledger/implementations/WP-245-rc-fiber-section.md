| **WP-245 — `section LadrunoNDFiber` (`LadrunoNDFiberSection3d`) and `nDMaterial LadrunoUniaxialFiber`** ([[LadrunoNDFiberSection3d_guide]]; MCFT shear-critical fibre-beam program #1131, rcbf-3) — A thin subclass of the stock `NDFiberSection3d` (fibre storage, `addFiber`, centroid and the fibre/patch/layer builders are stock) with a chosen shear-strain profile: `-shearProfile uniform [-alpha a]` is the stock √α rule (reproduces `NDFiber` to roundoff); `-shearProfile parabolic -shearDepth hy hz` imposes `γxy = 1.5(1 − (2y/hy)²) γy` and `γxz = 1.5(1 − (2z/hz)²) γz` (Petrangeli–Pinto–Ciampi, as Model C, centroid-relative, no renormalisation). Resultant `-shearResultant modelC` (default) takes `V = Σ τ A` as Model C does: not work-conjugate, `K_VV = GA` elastically (k = 1), **unsymmetric** tangent (needs an unsymmetric solver); `conjugate` takes `V = Σ τ φ A`: symmetric, `K_VV → 6/5 GA` (a kinematic parabola stiffens; Timoshenko's 5/6 needs a parabolic stress, which a fibre section cannot impose — the plan's "k = 5/6" gate was replaced by these closed forms). `-shearProfile` is required; a fibre outside the parabolic band is refused at the first state determination; DDM sensitivities are refused under the parabolic profile. 3D only (`ndm 3`). `LadrunoUniaxialFiber $tag $uniTag [-rho ρ]`: any `UniaxialMaterial` as a BeamFiber/BeamFiber2d fibre, axial strain → `σ(ε)`, zero shear stress and stiffness (no dowel, as Model C); other views refused. Shear map header-only in `LadrunoFiberShearKernel.h`. Wire: the stock payload plus a `Vector(7)` profile record (schema 1); the adapter sends its uniaxial material by broker.
