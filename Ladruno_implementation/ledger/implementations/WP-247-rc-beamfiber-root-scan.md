| **WP-247 — `LadrunoRCConcrete` BeamFiber view: Model C's root scan when the lateral Newton stalls** ([[LadrunoRCConcrete_guide]] §12.1; MCFT shear-critical fibre-beam program #1131, found running rcbf-4) — Where `ρ fyt < f_cr` (every specimen of the program's lab set), `r_y(εyy) = σyy + ρ f_s` is not monotone past the tension peak, and on the crack-onset step the view's lateral Newton could cycle on the falling branch and refuse the trial, although a root sits next to the committed strain (a Sezen–Moehle 2CLD12 force-based column stopped at 0.22 % drift there). When Newton fails, `driveBeamFiber` now samples one lateral normal (y, then z) at the committed value ± geometric offsets `[1e-8, 1e-1]`, takes the increasing sign change of that direction's residual nearest the committed value, bisects it (60 halvings), and balances the other two lateral unknowns by a 2×2 Newton at every sample; the point is accepted on the stalled-Newton balance test, so a bracket around a jump still refuses. The scan walks outward from the committed value and stops at the first bracket; the inner solve is capped at 12 iterations and 4 halvings and warm-started from the previous sample, and the whole scan at 3000 return maps, past which the trial refuses as before. New opt-in `-lateralNoRoot {refuse|modelC}` (default `refuse`, schema v9): where no balanced root exists (a cover fibre without ties after a shear failure, on a shear reversal: `σyy < 0` up to the box end; a Sezen–Moehle 3CLH18 force-based column stopped on its first unloading after the shear failure), `modelC` keeps Model C's rule, bisecting `r_y` over the nearest increasing y-bracket or else over `[−0.004, 0.15]`, with `εzz`, `γyz` balanced and `r_y` left unbalanced; response `lateralRoot` (0 Newton, 1 scan, 2 `modelC`); `LadrunoRCFiniteStrain` refuses the option. The lateral unknowns are kept in Model C's box (`εyy, εzz ∈ [−0.01, 0.15]`, `γyz ∈ ±0.15`; Newton iterates and scan samples are projected): a cover fibre fully cracked in y had run to `εyy ≈ 3e4` on a near-singular Jacobian (2CHD12, high axial load). Newton-reached states inside the box are byte-identical; the kernel signature gains optional `bool* rootScan` and `bool* noRootAccepted` out-flags (defaulted, every other caller unchanged). Gates: `tests/_testbed/rc_rootscan_gpp.cpp` (a lightly tied core fibre with Model C's envelopes, β, `sum` crack check, ties in y: R1 a 60-step cracking ramp converges on all steps, the scan running on the crack-onset step only; R2 every step against nested bracketing bisection from the same committed state 2e-16; R3 γxz with ties in z identical 0; R4 a 120-step ramp through cracking converges throughout, 2e-10; R5 a cover fibre on a tension–crush–unload path stays in the box on the reference root, 7e-13 in εyy, and fails at εyy = 3.3e4 with the box removed; R6 a cover fibre on a shear reversal: `refuse` stops at step 129 of 180, `modelC` converges on all 180 at Model C's rule written out, 0 in εyy, `|σyy|` left 0.029 MPa), wrapper `tests/test_ladrunoRCConcrete_rootscan_cpp.py`; the WP-243/245 g++ gates (`rc_beamfiber_gpp`, `rc_section_gpp`, `rc_spatial_gpp`) pass unchanged. | kernel fix (no new class) | — (33015 unchanged) | `SRC/material/nD/LadrunoRCKernel.h`, `SRC/material/nD/LadrunoRCConcrete.{h,cpp}`, `SRC/material/nD/LadrunoRCFiniteStrain.cpp`, `SRC/material/LadrunoOptSpec.h`, `tests/_testbed/rc_rootscan_gpp.cpp`, `tests/test_ladrunoRCConcrete_rootscan_cpp.py`, `Ladruno_implementation/LadrunoRCConcrete_guide.md` | draft | #1139 |
