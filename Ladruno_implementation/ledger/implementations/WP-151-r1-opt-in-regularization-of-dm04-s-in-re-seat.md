| **WP-151 (R1) — opt-in regularization of DM04's α_in re-seat singularity in SAS-ME (`IntScheme 129`)** ([[151_sanisand_reseat_singularity]]; [[LadrunoSANISAND_implex_guide]] §13.4). The exact DM04 rate equations have a Zeno accumulation of α_in re-seats near the peak (b:n → 0⁺, \|dα/dt\| → ∞ in finite pseudo-time). SAS-ME's `loadingNonPosH` wall refusals are its discrete image, one-to-one on the WP-138 wall states. Three independent flags, all OFF by default and then byte-identical (643 replays vs a pre-WP-151 build): `-sasHFloor c_A` (h = b0/max((α−α_in):n, c_A√(2/3)m)), `-sasReseatHyst c_rev` (re-seat only when (α−α_in):n < −c_rev√(2/3)m), `-sasSoftCap κ` (b:n < 0: H ≥ κX). Recommended together, c_A = c_rev = 1, κ = 0.5: the wall fan goes from 102/320 refused to 0/320 (floor alone 87, hysteresis alone 102). Calibrated element behaviour is unchanged (monotonic ≤ 2.7e-4·q_max, cyclic N identical). Census `sasStats` += hFloored, hSoftCapped, reseatHeld; WP-130's `sasOptions` response (33101) += hFloor, reseatHyst, softCap (indices 6–8). The oracle prototype (a copy of WP-134's with the toggles) is in the testbed.
