| **WP-246 — `LadrunoRCConcrete`: idempotent plastic-damage return (committed-surface consistency shift)** ([[LadrunoRCConcrete_guide]] §2, §22; MCFT shear-critical fibre-beam program #1131; found building WP-245) — Holding a strain kept relaxing the stress of a cracked fibre (BeamFiber view, Model C strip 35: −79600 → −79264 → −79015 N at a fixed strain), so the database round trip disagreed too. The cause was in `returnMap3D`, not in the view: the return rescales the tensile and compressive cones by different factors (`1 − dt_plastic`, `1 − dc_plastic`), and the Lubliner measure of a mixed-sign stress is not homogeneous in either cone, so the committed effective stress sat outside its own tensile surface. Re-entry at the committed strain then re-advanced `xt` (a fixed-point drift, geometric in the number of holds), and every loading step over-advanced it by the same error, so a ramp depended on its step count. Inherited verbatim from `ASDConcrete3D::compute` (upstream unchanged; quirk WP-246). Fix: the committed overshoot `(x_n − x_pl,n) − f(σ̃_n)/E`, when negative, is taken off the trial measure, so the threshold advances by the increment of the measure from the committed state; a committed state inside its surface is untouched, the shift is a constant of the step (no tangent term), IMPL-EX's explicit pass is unchanged, no wire change. The TS `tsScale` latch, the lateral Newton and the ties were ruled out (the re-solve at the committed lateral strain moved `xt` with zero lateral iterations). Mirrored in the Python oracle `tests/_testbed/rc_shell_ref.py`. `test_interlock_off_matches_asd_on_shear_path` now holds the ASD byte-faithful bound (1e-5) while the stress has one sign and 3e-2 once a compressive principal appears (measured 2 %; RC matches ASD to 1e-7 on the single-sign leg).
