| **WP-265 (D-IMPLEX) — tangent gates that bite** — **Diagnosis:** every family's IDENT floor was 0.0 because IDENT was never measured (`ladruno_mutation.yml` builds ZERO and SCALE only) and the header guessed "Newton usually still converges". A static read of the suites predicts the opposite: under K = I, 0.39–0.45 (CONTINUUM), 0.52–0.57 (SHELLMOD) and 0.70–0.75 (CPML) of the tests die, mostly because stiff static Newton diverges (dU = R). Divergence proves that a tangent exists, not that it is right. MPM's IDENT is a different mutant (the u-p coupling cut), measured 14/14 by hand. UP, CONTACT, EXPLICIT and SANISAND have no call sites. **(1) K-vs-FD gates at a plastic, history-dependent state** (one element, every DOF imposed, two committed steps, then the probe): `tests/test_continuum_tangent_gate.py` covers LadrunoBrick std / bbar / eas / ssp / uri and LadrunoBrick20 on LadrunoJ2 (Voce + 2 AF). Measured max\|K − K_fd\|/max\|K\| = 7.2e-11 / 1.4e-10 / 1.6e-8 / 1.1e-10 / 6.9e-11 / 3.1e-11. `tests/test_shellmod_tangent_gate.py` runs ShellMITC4 on LadrunoShellModifier (8 distinct non-unit modifiers) over a 4-layer LayeredShell(LadrunoJ2): 2.0e-7. Gate 1e-5; an identity tangent misses by 0.77–0.99 and the elastic one by 0.28–0.63. **(2) `mutation_gate.py`:** the IDENT suite (`paths_by_mode`) is each family's suite plus its gate file; the gate files stay out of ZERO and SCALE. The IDENT floors are provisional and set under the predicted bounds (CONTINUUM 0.30, SHELLMOD 0.40, CPML 0.55, MPM 0.90), to be replaced by the measured scores. `tests/test_mutation_tangent_gate_registry.py` pins all of this. **(3) IMPL-EX tangent-identity gate** (`tests/ladruno_implex_tangent_gate_check.cpp` + `tests/test_implex_tangent_gate_cpp.py`, g++, no build). For LadrunoJ2 and LadrunoUniaxialJ2, on 4 damage paths with the classes' own history threading (D~ = D_n + dD_n), the shipped IMPL-EX tangent equals the `Dual` derivative of the extrapolated map, with the clock latched, to ≤ 1.7e-13 (gate 1e-12). The value part is bit-identical, and the damage committed under IMPL-EX is the implicit one, bit for bit. Two planted mutants with unchanged stress value are flagged on every relevant step: a live damage D(ε) (min miss 1.3e-5) and an unlatched strain clock (min miss 2.3e-6), against a floor of 1e-7. **(4) `SRC/Ladruno_mutation.h`:** the IDENT doc is corrected, including where IDENT also moves a residual (Rayleigh βK, CriticalTimeStep, shell drilling, ASDShellQ4 EAS).
