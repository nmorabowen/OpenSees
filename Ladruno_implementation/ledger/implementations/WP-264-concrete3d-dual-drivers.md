| **WP-264 (D-C3D) — Concrete3D driver gradients and RebarBuckling `dfactor/dε` by dual numbers** — Two finite differences shipped inside production tangents are replaced by exact dual derivatives (program #1174, `LadrunoDual.h` from WP-262). **(1) `LadrunoConcrete3DKernel.h`:** the damage drivers are templated on the scalar (`invariantsT`, `equivStrainGeneralT`, `alphaCompressionT`, `tcPhiT`, `damageDriversT`, `compressiveEquivStrainT`, `scalarDriverOfPrincipals`); every double entry point keeps its name, signature and operations. `damagedTangent` now differentiates ε̃, x_s, α_c and ε̃_c off the hydrostatic axis by `dscalarDsigDual`: one `Dual<3>` pass seeded on the principal values that `damagedTangent` already holds, lifted by the spectral chain rule Σ_a (∂f/∂w_a) v_a⊗v_a (shear slots ×2). It replaces `dscalarDsig`, a central FD of 12 driver calls with 12 `eig3sym` per gradient at step `DRIVER_FD_REL·S`. No dual goes through `eig3sym`: every driver is a symmetric function of the principal values, so a repeated pair has equal partials and its term is the eigenspace projector, whatever basis `eig3sym` returned. The Lode angle is never differentiated: `lodeRT` carries dr/d(cos 3θ) in closed form (`drDcos3thetaRegularized`), finite on the meridians; the `|c3| ≤ 1` clamp clamps the value only. The cone vertex of ε̃ (`rad == 0`, which ε̃_c reaches in plane-stress tension) keeps the linear part's derivative. The axis keeps `dscalarDsigAxis` (one-sided FD along the axis). The FD path stays selectable for one WP: `-DLADRUNO_C3D_DRIVER_GRAD_FD=1`, or `driverGradFD()` per thread in the testbeds. **(2) `LadrunoRebarBuckling`:** the GA factor moves to the new `LadrunoRebarBucklingKernel.h` (`gaFactorT<T>`); `applyBucklingGA` takes `dfactor/dε` from one `Dual<1>` pass (`gaFactorDeriv`) instead of the central FD (h = 1e-8). **Gates (g++ -O2, no -march=native):** (a) `tests/_testbed/concrete3d_dual_check.cpp` (`tests/test_ladrunoConcrete3D_dual_cpp.py`): the double entry points are bit-identical to a verbatim copy of the pre-WP code (131 680 compares), and every dual value equals the double one. The dual gradient equals a step-plateau FD in MPa, kPa and Pa, off the kinks, max 4.1e-7 (p90 ≤ 1e-7), on random states, exactly repeated pairs, near-repeated pairs and the 356 replay-record states. It is finite on every off-axis state and basis-independent inside repeated pairs (1.1e-12), and matches an eigen-free `Dual<6>` pass (7.1e-11). Four mutants are flagged: shear ×2, Lode derivative, eigenpair scrambling, naive acos (NaN on 56 of 80 meridian states). `returnMap`'s stress and State are bit-identical between driver modes; the dual damaged tangent equals the FD of the damaged map to ≤ 3.3e-7 on three damage paths × 3 unit systems, and is never worse than the FD-driver tangent on the 328 record passes. (b) `concrete3d_kernel_check.cpp`: ALL PASS in both modes; the FD-mode output is byte-identical to the pre-WP header's. (c) The replay testbed built in FD mode re-records all 28 records byte-identically to the pre-WP header (mingw-ucrt). (d) `tests/test_ladrunoRebarBuckling_dual_cpp.py`: factor bit-identical (28 800), dual `dfactor/dε` = plateau FD to 2.2e-7, the GA tangent moves ≤ 3.7e-10, and two mutants are flagged. **Speed:** a driver gradient drops from 22–27 µs (FD) to 0.04–0.42 µs (dual), 140× for the three that every damaged tangent takes. On damage-advancing states, `returnMap` with the tangent drops from 184 to 121 µs; the tangent's share drops from 119 to 56 µs. **Replay:** the kernel blob changes, so both fixtures need a re-record. In dual mode, 6 of 14 C records move their D line (≤ 8.0e-9 rel.), and 7 of 10 R records (the plane-stress σ₂₂ Newton iterates on D₂₂) move stress and outcome. C and F records keep their stress bit for bit.
