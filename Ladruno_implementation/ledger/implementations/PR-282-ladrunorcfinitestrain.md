| **LadrunoRCFiniteStrain** — finite-strain (Hencky) view of the RC plastic-damage material ([[19_ladruno_rc_shell_adr]] Phase 4b; `nDMaterial`, a `FiniteStrainNDMaterial` subclass driven by `setTrialF(F)`, consumed by `element LadrunoBrick -geom finite`).
