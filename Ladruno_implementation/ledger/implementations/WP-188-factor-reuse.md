| **WP-188 — factorization reuse across time steps** ([[188_ladruno_factor_reuse_adr]]) — `algorithm KrylovNewton ... -reuseFactor m ?-reuseIter k? ?-reuseRate r? ?-reuseReport n?`: a step may start on the LU of an earlier step's tangent (no formTangent; MUMPS job 3 only), Krylov-accelerated. Triggers: step age > m, dt change (1e-8 rel), SOE/integrator/size change, `domainChanged()`, k iterations or test-norm ratio > r on a kept factor (form at the current iterate, restart the subspace), and failure of a step that started on a kept factor (undo `update(-ΣΔU)`, form at the predictor, retry once as plain KrylovNewton). Same convergence test; converged states differ from full Newton at the tolerance scale (tighten ~10x for result-neutral runs). Every decision `MPI_Allreduce(MAX)` over MPI_COMM_WORLD in a per-target TU; statistics line on rank 0 (destructor, `-reuseReport`, `print -algorithm` on every rank).
