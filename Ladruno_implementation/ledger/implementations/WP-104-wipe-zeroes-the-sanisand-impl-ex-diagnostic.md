| **WP-104 — `wipe` zeroes the SANISAND IMPL-EX diagnostic ledger** (guide [[LadrunoSANISAND_implex_guide]] §6; quirk entry "`wipe` zeroes `implexRefusals`..." in [[LEDGER_quirks]]). **The defect (apeGmsh live test `test_ladruno_sanisand_selfweight_implex_live.py`, 2026-09-15, tip `634824e1f`):** `implexRefusals` slots 0-3 and 5, all of `implexGuards`, and `avgImplexError` live in ONE anonymous-namespace singleton (`LadrunoImplexGlobals`, `LadrunoSANISAND.cpp`) that nothing in `wipe` reached, so a FRESH `LadrunoSANISAND` in a NEW model (new tag, no `analyze()` yet) read `[9,0,0,9,0,9]` — the companion refusals an earlier model in the process had latched — and the guide's end-of-run `implexRefusals[3] == 0` discipline was process-order-dependent. Only slot 4 (`commitLatched`) is per-instance. **The fix:** `LadrunoImplexGlobals::resetOnWipe()` + the free function `ladrunoSanisandResetImplexGlobals()` (declared in `LadrunoSANISAND.h`), called from `OPS_clearAllNDMaterial()` — the hook all three wipe paths (`commands.cpp`, `OpenSeesCommands.cpp`, `PythonAnalysisBuilder.cpp`) share. Resets the refusal ledger, the post-latch count, the guard census, the error accumulators AND the `firstCommitter` commit-round marker (dangling after a wipe). **Deliberately NOT reset:** `ops.reset()`/`revertToStart()` (same model — legs are read as deltas, per the 2026-09-07 quirk, now amended) and the 10-per-process `opserr` throttles.
