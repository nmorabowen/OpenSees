| **WP-252 — `section LadrunoNDFiber … -tangent numerical [-tangentStep d]`** ([[LadrunoNDFiberSection3d_guide]] §3b; MCFT shear-critical fibre-beam program #1131, found running rcbf-4) — Opt-in forward-difference section tangent: each of the six section deformations is perturbed so the largest fibre strain it moves changes by `d` (default 1e-7), every fibre is re-evaluated (its BeamFiber lateral balance included) and then restored at the base deformation, and the six resultants are differenced; a column whose perturbed sweep a fibre refuses keeps its analytic values. Why: `LadrunoRCConcrete`'s tangent is the damaged secant `W_B C0`, positive by construction; past a shear failure a force-based column of the lab set (Lynn et al. 1996, 3CLH18) lengthens as it loses strength under a constant axial load (negative axial stiffness), and with the secant the global Newton diverged monotonically (about 1.3x per iteration) at any displacement step, arc length included. With `-tangent numerical`, plain Newton crossed that step. The resultants are unchanged (bit-identical on the RC cracking path of the tests); the cost is seven fibre sweeps per state determination. The restore is exact when the fibres' `setTrialStrain` is idempotent from the committed state (WP-246 for `LadrunoRCConcrete`). Wire schema 1 → 2 (`Vector(10)`); the classic Tcl builder passes the option through.
