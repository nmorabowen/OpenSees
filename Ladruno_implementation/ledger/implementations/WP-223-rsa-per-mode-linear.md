| **WP-223 — `responseSpectrumPerMode -linear`** ([[214_ladruno_rsa_per_mode_output_adr]] D10) — opt-in `-linear <-linearTol $tol> <-linearCheck ($m.. \| all)>`: each mode is evaluated once, at the direction of largest \|Γ\|, and the reactions / reaction-set / element-group outputs of the other directions are Γ_d / Γ_ref × the reference (exact zeros where Γ_d = 0); 2 element updates per mode instead of 2 per (direction, mode). Inertia stays per direction. Self-check: the check modes (default the first with an amplitude) are also evaluated at −½ the amplitude; −2 × that response must match the reference to `-linearTol` (default 1e-6) of each component's summed term magnitude (floored at 1e-6 of the total-force terms), else the call is refused (no silent fallback) and the domain is left committed. Output adds `linearCheckModes`, `linearCheckError`, `linearTol`. Without the flag the output is byte-identical to the build before (golden `tests/data/wp223_rsa_flagoff_golden.json`, recorded on d56fbfc48, gated on a modal-input fingerprint). Gate `tests/test_wp223_rsa_per_mode_linear.py`: flag-on = flag-off to 1e-9 on ASDShellQ4 towers (with / without `-pDelta`, Linear and PDelta frames, over a gravity preload + `loadConst`) and to 1e-12 on Linear elastic frames; Γ_d / Γ_ref scaling bit-exact; refusals on Corotational frames under preload, a symmetric Steel01 spring past yield, and a pure PDelta-geomTransf frame (dP·dΔ, ledger quirk WP-223); Tcl parity. `tests/_testbed/rsa_tower.py` gains `transf` / `shell_args`, `tests/bench_wp216_rsa_per_mode.py` gains `--linear`, `--preload`, `--transf`, `--pdelta-shells`.
