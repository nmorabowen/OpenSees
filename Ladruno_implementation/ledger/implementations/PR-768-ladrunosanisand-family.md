| **LadrunoSANISAND family** ([[86_ladruno_sanisand_adr]]) — `LadrunoSANISAND`/`LadrunoSANISAND3D`/`LadrunoSANISANDPlaneStrain`, a thin subclass of `ManzariDafalias` (Ghofrani/Arduino, U. Washington, after Dafalias & Manzari 2004). PR-1's only difference from vanilla: the two low-stress constants `m_Presidual` and `m_Pmin`, hardcoded unconditionally inside `ManzariDafalias::initialize()`, become **optional constructor arguments, carried on the wire (`sendSelf`/`recvSelf`), and echoed at construction** (defaults `0.0` and `1.0e-3*P_atm`, per NTUASand02 — a cohesionless sand has no cohesion). Exists because the ADR §3 source audit found vanilla's `m_Presidual` has **no wire slot** (`Vector data(97)` carries `m_Pmin` at `data(96)`, nothing for `m_Presidual`): the null-constructor broker path sets `m_P_atm=0` before `initialize()` runs, so an `OpenSeesMP` worker or a database-restored material silently runs at `p_r = 0` while the serial process beside it runs at 1.01 kPa — a silent-wrong-answer defect at low confinement (own-bounding-surface departure +20% at p₀=1 kPa), not a robustness one. `-honorTolR` accepted only `0` in PR-1; **PR-3 wires it** to the `mHonorTolRInME` seam PR-2 opened in vanilla, so it now takes `0|1` (0 = vanilla's hardcoded `TolE = 1e-4` in `ModifiedEuler`, 1 = this deck's `TolR`). D5b (the `D_factor` sigmoid's **units**) was repaired in vanilla by PR-2. D5a (its **shape**) and §7.3 (the `m_e_init` vs current-`e` elastic modulus) remain **deferred** — both are formulation changes, not error fixes, and PR-3 measured rather than took them ([[86_ladruno_sanisand_pr3_tripwire_memo]]).
