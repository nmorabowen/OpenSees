| **WP-230 — SAS-ME `-sasLowP clamp p_floor p_sep p_contact` (+ `-sasLowPAlpha leave\|project`): a low-pressure clamp, the comparison mode to WP-152's separation (TIMs F25)** ([[230_sanisand_lowp_clamp]]; [[LadrunoSANISAND_implex_guide]] §13.7). A second mode of WP-152's per-point state machine in `ladrunoSasIntegrate`, with ONE thing changed: a point that loses its confinement is held at p = p_floor and KEEPS its last committed deviator, frozen, instead of going to p_min·I. Triggers (E1 tension, E2 accuracy/cost at p0 < p_sep), their gates, the continuous closing law and the volumetric re-contact are WP-152's. Exclusive with `-sasTensionCutoff`; refused wherever the cutoff is. α of a clamped point: `leave` (default: as committed; the state is outside the yield cone and that is measured) or `project` (closest point of the cone around the clamped stress); at re-contact α is projected and α_in := α under both. The stress change is never added to α. Oracle first: a driver around the unmodified WP-134 oracle, whose separation mode reproduces WP-152's driver record for record. `sasStats` 44 → 46 (`clampMaxFRatio`, `clampMaxBound`; entries/exits/active are WP-152's columns, shared); `sasOptions` 12 → 15; wire tag 230.0.
