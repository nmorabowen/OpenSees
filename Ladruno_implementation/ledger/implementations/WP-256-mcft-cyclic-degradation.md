| **WP-256 — `LadrunoMCFTStrip -cyclicDegradation $Ccyc [$m] [-cyclicDriver tension or both]`** ([[LadrunoMCFTStrip_guide]] §8a; MCFT shear-critical fibre-beam program #1131, improvement I2) — Model C's strut remembers only the maximum principal strains, so repeated cycles to one drift retrace the same envelope; against the lab tests (Sezen & Moehle 2002; Lynn et al. 1996) the columns with closely spaced ties fail too late and keep too much residual strength. The option adds one committed memory, the cumulative crack RE-opening `P` (the increases of `(e1 − ecr)+` that stay below its previous maximum, weighted by the opening reached to the power `m`, default 0; a monotonic path and elastic cycles add exactly zero; `-cyclicDriver both` also counts the strut's re-compression above `0.5 ep` below its previous maximum), and multiplies the strut stress by `β_cyc = 1/(1 + Ccyc P)` on top of `β(e1)`: a damage indicator on the compression response driven by crack cycling, in the spirit of Palermo & Vecchio (2003). Implicit: the trial adds its own re-opening increment, so the damage enters the `eps_y` solve and the forward-difference tangent, and commit stores what the converged trial used (a committed state re-solves to itself). Default `0` = Model C's law bit for bit; every WP-254 gate unchanged. Response `cyclicDamage` = `(P, β_cyc)`; wire Vector 28 → 34. Not calibrated: on the column battery it moves the closely tied columns to the test's failure drift but over-degrades the widely tied ones, so `Ccyc` is to be chosen together with a dowel term.
