| **WP-193 — cost-aware refactorization trigger** ([[193_ladruno_reuse_cost_trigger_adr]]) — `algorithm KrylovNewton ... -reuseFactor m -reuseCost on ?-reuseCostSafety s? ?-reuseCostWarmup w? ?-reuseCostProbe p?`: measures wall time of a factorization (t_F) and of an iteration on a kept factor (t_I), the iterations with a fresh tangent (n_F, EMA) and on a kept factor vs age (n_K(a) = c0 + c1 a, forgetting least squares); keeps a factor at a step start iff s·n_K(S) ≤ A(S) = (t_F/t_I + n_F + Σ n_K)/S (age-replacement rule), refreshes in a step once (i − n_K(a))·t_I ≥ t_F (ski rental); WP-188 triggers stay as hard limits; probe keep after p refusals. Timings MPI_Allreduce(MAX)-reduced per step (rank-identical model and decisions). Off by default and byte-identical to WP-188 when off. With it on, runs are not bit-reproducible (decisions depend on measured time).
