| **ADR-75 P1j — the factorization-share size trend, measured** ([[75_ladruno_sparse_direct_strategy_adr]] §9) — closes P1h's one open follow-up, which had *predicted* the trend rather than measured it. Lane-B cube over **11,520 → 136,080 DOF** (n=15/20/25/30/35), 15 steps, median of 2, both storage modes; solve counts identical between modes at every size, tip displacement matching to <1e-9. **`fac/(fac+tri)` rises 71.4% → 93.7% (`-matrixType 2`) and 84.4% → 96.1% (`-matrixType 0`)**, and the mechanism is a measured **exponent gap of ~0.64–0.72**: `soe.factor` ~N^1.65–1.79 against `soe.trisolve` ~N^0.94–1.07 (`soe.symbolic` is *sublinear*, N^0.65–0.90, fading to 0.24% of step). **Consequence: factorization goes 15.0% → 56.2% of STEP while triangular solve — the part no reuse lever touches — shrinks 6.0% → 3.7%**, so `-krylov` / the `factored` gate / ModifiedNewton / IMPL-EX are worth ~1.18× at 11.5k DOF and ~2.28× at 136k. Extrapolating the fitted exponents to 540,675 DOF predicts a solver share saturating at ~100%, independently corroborating ADR-75b G-L3's measured 98.5%. **Two measurement traps found and banked** ([[LEDGER_quirks]]): (a) the Profiler is a process-global singleton that `ops.wipe()` does not reset, so the first sweep reported the SUM of all prior runs (`soe.factor` calls 44→88→…→936) as a clean monotone trend — invisible to any check that only reads the profiler, so `p1j_rollup.py` now REFUSES to print unless every profile's `step` agrees with an independently recorded wall clock; (b) benchmarking two configs in one process confounds storage mode with **allocation order** at large N — symmetric ran 1.58× faster in second position, which had made its assembly look 37% *slower* than unsymmetric at n=35 when warm measurement shows it is faster.
