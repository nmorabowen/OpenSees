`ProfilerResults.rollup()` now backfills the anchor's wall as the sum of its children (regression in `test_contract.py`). **Live console monitor** (#485, `Ladruno_tools/profiler_viewer/profiler_monitor.py`): `ProfilerMonitor` wraps an `analyze()` loop and prints per-step iterations/`testNorms`/dt, speed + ETA, memory growth and periodic phase shares from a throw-away mid-run `profiler('report')`; it runs on the shipped binary with no C++ change, and `monitor_smoke.py` is its binary-free regression.
