| **ADR-90 WP-A2 — the tau = 0 collapse-load band on softening SANISAND** ([[_adr90_tau0_qu_band]]) — the strategy critic's **UN-DESCOPE GATE** for ADR 90: does the UNREGULARIZED collapse load of a strip footing on softening `LadrunoSANISAND` already converge under mesh refinement? The fork had that answer for DruckerPrager (`tests/test_r3_prandtl_collapse_gate.py`, 1.0849 / 0.9977 / 0.9514, every leg a genuine plateau) and did not have it for SANISAND, whose non-associated **softening** is the reason ADR 90 exists. **No `SRC/` change and no build** — this measures ADR 90 sec.3.1 option (d), which that section itself calls "the negative control of the whole ADR, not an option". The driver **IMPORTS** the R3 gate's graded plane-strain strip mesh, its domain and its ADR-63 D16 stepping guards (including the pinned `SUBDIV_BUDGET = 80` and its calibration history) and Gorini's `_PARAMS`, rather than copying them. New here: the weighted / K0-geostatic staging SANISAND needs (it cannot be run weightless — `G ~ sqrt(p)` and the whole state is pressure-dependent, so `p -> 0` is a pathology, not a simplification); a **softening-aware capacity classifier** (R3's `DROP_TRUNCATE` is a bottomed-out-run detector on a plateau problem and a *signal deleter* on a softening one, so it is disabled by name; `PEAK` joins `TARGET`/`BUDGET` as an admissible termination mode while `FLOOR`/`WALL` stay seizure); **matched-settlement checkpoints**, so three meshes are compared at the SAME s/B even when they terminate at different ones; and the ADR-90 sec.7.3 threshold-free width metric, calibrated exactly (a k-element top hat reads `k*hx` to 1e-12, pinned by `sanisand_tau0_summary.py --selfcheck`). Controls per leg: the pinned engine hash asserted from `ops.ladrunoBuild()`; the resultant identity `sum R_z = gamma*V`; the **1-D geostatic stress patch** the resultant is structurally blind to (`00_canonical_testbed` sec.1d); the per-Gauss-point `eta/M_c` field at the stage flip plus an engine-log grep for `Outside Bounding` and the low-p `CLAMPING` notice; and the footing-vs-base reaction cross-check. **Found and fixed two deck defects that were measuring the solver rather than the soil:** `TanType` defaults to the **elastic** tangent in the parser (`LadrunoSANISAND.cpp:117`) while the null/parallel constructors default to 2, so every existing fork SANISAND deck runs `algorithm Newton` as de-facto modified Newton — invisible on a zero-free-DOF material-point deck, which is the shape of all of them, and ~7x on a boundary-value problem; and a tight `NormDispIncr` is **unreachable** on SANISAND (the substepped `ModifiedEuler` return makes the discrete map only piecewise smooth; the residual stalls at a median 6.6e-7 m) and is additionally not mesh-neutral, which disqualifies it inside a mesh-convergence study.
