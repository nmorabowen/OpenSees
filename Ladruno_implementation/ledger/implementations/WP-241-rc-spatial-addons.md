| **WP-241 — `LadrunoRCConcrete` / `LadrunoRCFiniteStrain`: `-addonPlane {membrane\|spatial}`, `-crackGate {strain\|damage}`, interlock tangent over all 6 columns, condensed `getInitialTangent`** ([[LadrunoRCConcrete_guide]] §5.1, §5.2; MCFT shear-critical fibre-beam program #1131, first WP) — (a) `-addonPlane spatial`: β, the TS floor and the cracked-ν latch read `ε1`/`p1` from the full 3×3 strain (`eps1Spatial`, multiplicity-aware: the dual, injection and measurement vectors average over the `m` eigenvectors that share λmax, `meas·inj = 1` for every `m`), so a beam fibre's γxz gets the same add-ons as its γxy; refuses `-interlock/-cyclic/-xcrack/-shearRetention` (the fixed crack is x–y only; the kernel also skips it). (b) `-crackGate damage` (spatial default): the latch and the TS floor open only once the tensile threshold `xt` is past the tension-backbone peak (latch on the committed `xt`, one-step lag; floor on the trial `xt`), so Poisson expansion in compression no longer opens them (the WP-241 probe: a plane-stress point latched at ≈0.42 f'c and diverged). (c) The `-interlock` 2a-capped and `-cyclic` tangents now remove the smeared crack-shear sensitivity in all 6 columns (was `{0,1,3}`: the ε_zz column kept ~E/8 of pinned-away stiffness in every condensed view). (d) `getInitialTangent`, `revertToStart` and `recvSelf` give the σ33 = 0 Schur complement in the PlaneStress/PlateFiber views (was the raw C0 block, λ + 2μ).
