| **MohrCoulombTensionCutoff — MC + Rankine tension-cutoff composite YF/PF for ASDPlasticMaterial3D** ([[84_ladruno_mc_tension_cutoff_adr]]) — consumer Cerro Lindo ADR-0005 M3 measured a horseshoe-cavity/EDZ geomaterial with an unbounded MC tensile intercept (275 vs a Hoek-Brown-derived 24.7 kPa, 498 EDZ exceedances at λ≥0.95) and 20 GPs measurably OUTSIDE the yield surface from the apex-region Newton exhaustion-accept. Adds a registered composite `f = max(f_MC, f_TC)` YF + associated-cutoff/non-associated-MC PF pairing (`TC_min_stress` input) plus ONE new opt-in integrator mechanism in `Backward_Euler` — a `yf_has_special_return` trait + `SPECIAL_RETURN` hook, `if constexpr`-gated so it compiles away for every existing YF (bit-identical by construction). The hook resolves every cutoff-active trial with EXACT principal-space returns (Clausen/Damkilde/Andersen taxonomy): Rankine face (`s3'=T`) and edge (`s2'=s3'=T`); MC∩TC Koiter two-multiplier corner (frozen-direction, non-associated MC ψ retained, falls to frozen-direction 1D MC Newton if λ_TC<0); compound corner MC∩TC(s3)∩TC(s2) closed-form `(s1*,T,T)` (the triaxial-extension-corner attractor for symmetric lateral-tension drives — without it those states collapse to the vertex, losing the entire MC confinement, measured −235.25 kPa of s1 replaced by +24.7); MC-dominant fall-through (hands to the untouched scalar Newton when `f_MC≥f_TC` and every exact cutoff feature rejects); apex/vertex terminal `T_eff·δ` (always feasible by construction — the escalation chain therefore has a structural zero-f>0 guarantee, terminating at the always-feasible apex). All hook tangents are Koiter-consistent per active set; the **raw** tangent is returned and the integrator applies the material's configured `tangent_type` to it (**ADR-84 P3** — P0 secant-blended inside the YF, which silently overrode `tangent_type` on every hook-resolved Gauss point and fabricated stiffness in the degenerate corner direction, 87% tangent error: the measured cause of the Cerro Lindo M5 confined-shear stall. Default `Secant` is byte-identical; `Continuum` now delivers the raw tangent and needs 3.5x fewer Newton iterations on the confined-corner battery. P3 also adds `return_quality`, so the terminal vertex projection is refused loudly under `strict_convergence` instead of silently committing a discarded deviatoric state). Registered as ONE explicit `COMBINED_MODELS` entry (`LinearIsotropic3D_EL × MohrCoulombTensionCutoff_YF × MohrCoulombTensionCutoff_PF × BackStress<NullHardeningTensorFunction>`, +1 instantiation 45→46) — deliberately NOT the cross product (a dilatant MC-flow-on-cutoff pairing is mechanically wrong and grows the monster TU).
