| **StagedStrainNDMaterial** (`nDMaterial StagedStrain`) — **small-strain, dimension-general (2D+3D), AUTO-CAPTURING** staged-activation wrapper: the small-strain sibling of `InitDefGrad`. Makes a **small-strain** continuum element (`FourNodeQuad`, `stdBrick`, plane-strain/stress, axisymmetric, tris/tets) **born stress-free at the deformed geometry** when appended mid-stage. **Fixes the three ways the upstream `InitStrain` bites staged use:** (1) `InitStrain` is **3D-only** (`getOrder()` hardcoded 6) and `exit(-1)`s on a non-3D inner (kernel kill) → `StagedStrain` is **order-general** (`ε0` sized to `inner->getOrder()`; `getCopy(type)` adapts the inner to PlaneStrain/PlaneStress/AxiSymm/3D) and **fails gracefully**; (2) `InitStrain`'s `ε0` is **fixed** → `StagedStrain` **auto-captures `ε0`** on the first `setTrialStrain` (= birth), so **one tag births a whole lift**, each per-GP copy snapshotting its own birth strain; (3) `InitStrain` **adds** a prestrain → `StagedStrain` **subtracts** the captured birth strain (`ε_rel = ε − ε0`) for stress-free birth. Born **genuinely virgin** (zero stress AND zero plastic history); tangent is an **exact passthrough** (`ε0` constant ⇒ no FD gate, unlike the finite wrapper). Additive ⇒ **exact for small strain only** (finite uses `StagedDefGrad`/`InitDefGrad`). **COMPOSES with the upstream `InitStrain`** for staged-birth-plus-prestrain by nesting `StagedStrain` **OUTERMOST**: `element → StagedStrain(InitStrain(real, ε_pre))` ⇒ born carrying exactly `σ(ε_pre)` (nest the other way and the prestrain is captured into `ε0` and cancels). Opt-out `-noInit` (≡ bare inner); explicit `-eps0 e1 e2 …`; `setResponse("eps0"/"totalStrain")`. **Also registers the `StagedDefGrad` alias for `InitDefGrad`** so the `Staged*` family reads coherently (`StagedStrain` small / `StagedDefGrad` finite).
