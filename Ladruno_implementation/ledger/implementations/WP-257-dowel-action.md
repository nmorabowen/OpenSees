| **WP-257 — `LadrunoUniaxialFiber -dowel $fc $fy $db $ls [-dowelK $kd] [-dowelSlip $su] [-dowelDecay $Cd]`** ([[LadrunoNDFiberSection3d_guide]] §4a; MCFT shear-critical fibre-beam program #1131, improvement I3) — Model C leaves out dowel action, so on the lab tests (Sezen & Moehle 2002; Lynn et al. 1996) the columns with widely spaced ties have no tie-independent shear mechanism. The option gives the bar fibre a shear stress per shear component from the crack slip `s = abs(γ) ls`: capacity per unit bar area `kd (4/π) sqrt(fc fy) sqrt(1 − (σ/fy)²)` (Dulácska 1972; Vintzeleou & Tassios 1987; zero once the bar yields), envelope `1 − exp(−3 s/su)` (95 % at `su`, default `0.05 db`), and an origin-oriented secant inside the largest committed slip (the pinched dowel loop), and an optional implicit cyclic decay `1/(1 + Cd D)`, `D` the cumulative re-slip below the previous maximum in units of `su` (a monotonic path adds exactly zero; `Cd` 0 = off). Exact tangent, including the axial coupling through the uniaxial tangent; initial tangent `3 τu ls/su`. With `-shearResultant modelC` the bar adds `A_b τ` to the section shear. Default off = no shear, as before; every WP-245 test unchanged. Response `dowel` = `(τy, τz, smax_y, smax_z, τu, D_y, D_z)`; wire: ID 4 → 5 (dowel flag) and, when on, one Vector(13). Header-only law `LadrunoDowelKernel.h`.
