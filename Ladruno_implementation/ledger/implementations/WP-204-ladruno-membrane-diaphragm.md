| **WP-204 `LadrunoMembrane` diaphragm** (YOO SLE port, program #1007) -- (1) The ADR-98 in-plane stiffness was a CST condensation onto the OUTER polygon only, with every other boundary vertex slaved to straight edges: hole-edge nodes and interior `-supportNodes` (columns, walls inside the panel) got ZERO in-plane stiffness (YOO probe t1: interior column ux = 0; membrane_raw2: no in-plane reaction at hole/support nodes). Now an LST (6-node) mesh of the panel is condensed EXACTLY (Guyan, sparse LDL' via Eigen, `LadrunoMembraneDiaphragm.h`) onto every external node: outer loop, every hole loop, `-supportNodes`, and the new **`-diaphragmNodes n...`** (in-plane only, no gravity share: beam nodes along an edge, wall nodes along a hole edge). Gravity distribution untouched (its own mesh). A frame end node lying on the panel that is not an element node gets a warning. Guyan mass condensed the same way (`-lumped`: HRZ on the condensed mass). Diaphragm mesh cap 6000 vertices (the kernel's 1200 silently coarsened `-meshSize` on large slabs). (2) **`-f11 -f22 -f12`** in-plane modifiers, D_ij * sqrt(f_i f_j) like `LadrunoShellModifier`; the full 3x3 initial tangent of a `-mat` material is now used (orthotropic plane-stress materials work), nonlinear scaling unchanged (D00 ratio). (3) Responses **`membraneForces`** (N11 N22 N12 per triangle, centroid, e1/e2 frame), **`meanMembraneForce`**, **`diaphragmForces`** (in-plane nodal force per node, global), **`diaphragmVertices`**, **`diaphragmTriangles`**; the material mean strain is the exact area mean of the condensed field (the stock boundary integral ignored holes).
