| **ADR-75 P1h — PARDISO phase-split harness + published split** ([[75_ladruno_sparse_direct_strategy_adr]] §9) — closes the MEASUREMENT half of the instrumentation item that PR #667 opened: #667 added the 7 brackets and CI proved they *exist*, but nothing published what the split *is*, and that split is what bounds every reuse lever in the lane. `p1h_parse_split.py` extends `lane3/parse_lane3.py` (which only knew the three UmfPack-era names) with `soe.cgs` / `dc.s.fill` / `dc.s.verify` / deep `soe.addA`, summing each over EVERY site per the `LEDGER_quirks` multi-site rule. `sweep_p1h.sh` interleaves 5 configs × 3 rounds + 2 deep rounds, and carries two guards the earlier sweeps lacked: it **aborts if any compiler is running** (walls would be junk) and **aborts if the `.pyd` lacks the `soe.cgs` string**, because a stale binary otherwise yields a full table of zeros — the exact failure mode this whole item exists to fix. **Measured (Lane B, 11 520 DOF, median of 3):** factorization is **~88% of solver time @1T and UmfPack and PARDISO agree to 0.6 pp** — PARDISO's win is a faster factorization, not a different phase mix; but **the share is not a constant** — threading (2.20× factor vs 1.48× trisolve) and half-storage (2.00× vs 1.25×) both speed factorization ~2× more than substitution, dropping %fac 87.4 → 81.8 → **73.4**, which is the direct measurement of what P1e could only infer from a size trend and is Amdahl *inside* the solver explaining the 1→8-thread 1.58× ceiling. At the shipped config (`-matrixType 2` @4T) factorization is **16.0% of step** = the hard ceiling on any factorization-reuse lever at this size, and trisolve 4.4% is untouchable by them. `-krylov` drops `soe.factor` from 44 calls to 1 and **demonstrates the caveat concretely**: `%fac` reads 5.0% while 77.4% sits in `soe.cgs`, which is where a CGS give-up's refactorization would hide.
