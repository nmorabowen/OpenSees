| **WP-194 — mixed-precision MUMPS** ([[194_ladruno_mumps_mixed_precision_adr]]) — `system Mumps ... -precision single ?-refine k?` (alias `-mixed`): the distributed double tangent is copied to float and factored by SMUMPS; the RHS (scaled by its inf-norm) goes through the float factor and comes back as a double correction; assembly, residual and the Newton update stay double, so Newton is an inexact Newton on the exact residual. `-refine k` (default 0) adds k in-solver refinement steps against the double matrix (distributed matvec + `MPI_Reduce`). No DMUMPS instance is created in single mode. Coexists with `-ICNTL7/-ICNTL14`, `-BLR`/`-ICNTL35`/`-CNTL7` (BLR compresses the float factor), `-stats` (unchanged header + a `precision` line, WP-190 timing labels, residual history) and WP-188 `-reuseFactor` (the kept factor is the float one). `sendSelf`/`recvSelf` carry precision/refine for SP subordinates. Fail closed: bad value, `-refine` without single, serial MUMPS, or a build without libsmumps refuse the command. CMake `LADRUNO_MUMPS_SINGLE` AUTO/ON/OFF detects `smumps.lib`/`libsmumps.a` + `smumps_c.h` and defines `_LADRUNO_SMUMPS`.
