### LadrunoConcrete3D: the adaptive sub-incremented return reported the LAST piece's tangent; the damaged tangent micro-FD'd the return map (apeGmsh #2621 F2, ported onto #877, [#1050](https://github.com/nmorabowen/OpenSees/pull/1050))
- **Bites (before F2, on #877's kernel):** (1) `-subIncr adaptive` (and the deterministic mode's adaptive rescue) returned the last piece's one-piece consistent tangent of an N-piece return (O(1) wrong once N >= 2; F2 measured p50 0.45 at 2 pieces on the d14fb6958 corbel record dumps). The deterministic default already chained its pieces (`PieceSens`, #877 review M1). (2) `damagedTangent` took the beta_c, ctTemper-proj and tcTemper-proj gradients by composite micro-FDs THROUGH the return map (`rmForFD`, up to 36 maps per tangent). (3) At the tension apex (rho_bar = 0) the per-component micro-FD of the damage drivers averaged two meridian slopes: the damaged tangent was 0.9-2.8 off its FD on every apex step of a hydrostatic-tension drive. (4) A bilinear omega_t (or CDPM2-drive omega_c) exactly AT `OMEGA_MAX` fell through to the legacy exponential IFT branch. (5) On a tension-DEAD point with n >= 2 deterministic pieces the cut's effective tangent used d/d(deps) C^-1 of the zero-increment chain where it needs d/d(start): 2.0-4.4 off its FD (a #877 defect found while verifying the port).
- **Now:** every sub-incremented path reports the chain rule over its converged pieces (`chainPieceSens`; Perez-Foguet, Rodriguez-Ferran & Huerta, CMAME 2001) and its kappa row `dkpDeps = d kp/d eps` (`returnMapTensor(..., dkpDeps)`, through `effectiveReturn`'s cut and Duvaut-Lions blend). `damagedTangent` runs no return map (closed forms; `returnMapTensor` is declared after it, so a call is a compile error; kernel check E4 counts 0). The apex gradient is taken along the axis (`dscalarDsigAxis`, unit-free). The cap is flat. The cut differentiates its zero-increment map with respect to its start (`wrtStart`). `returnMapTensorSchedule` integrates a given schedule. The per-piece IFT is the derivative of whichever root the piece returned (vertex, plain Newton, the below-root or nested-scan root, `newtonGlob`): every accepted hardening root solves R1..R4.
- **Stress and state are bit-exact to #877 at the `returnMap` material point** (57 600 driven steps: 3 modes x 2 flows x 3 ctTemper x 2 tcTemper x 2 Hp x 2 eta x 8 paths). **The confined fibre view moves** (its lateral Newton reads Ceff): 5 856 of 138 240 steps differ, max |dsigma|/max(|sigma|, ft) 3.6e-10 where both converge; refusals: 0 change in the deterministic default and in `refuse`; +456 in `-subIncr adaptive` with the legacy flow, where the adaptive map is discontinuous (direct first) and the lateral Newton, on the correct chain tangent, 3-cycles across the direct/sub-stepped boundary that the last-piece tangent happened to jump over. The plane-stress sigma_22 Newton reads the damaged D22, so it moves in every mode: on 3 in-plane 300-step drives x 16 material variants per mode, 41 of 48 drives change in each mode; in every drive whose refusals change (20 deterministic, 16 adaptive) the FIRST change is a step #877 refused and the port accepts. Before it, converged stresses differ by up to 3.5e-3 (deterministic: the n-boundary jumps, the eps22 iterates differ) and 2.8e-4 (adaptive).
- **Workaround/status:** verify a sub-incremented tangent against an FD of the map with its schedule FROZEN (kernel check E1/E1d, `returnMapPieces` + `Params::subIncrForceN`, oracle `return_map_schedule` / `consistent_tangent_kp`, which follow the mode). Remaining FD residuals: effective-layer direct returns up to 1.4e-4 (identical in #877); the per-piece kappa row (#877's `PieceSens`, unchanged; cause not isolated) up to 3e-3 relative against an FD of kp in uniaxial compression, while the stress tangents there stay at the effective-layer level; and tension-dead cracked points with ctTemper proj and Hp 0.5 whose nominal stress carries ~1e-3 MPa noise (their FD grows like 1/h, so it is no reference there). The driver-gradient step is relative (#2639, folded into F2 at 7fe4cb121): `dscalarDsig` and `dscalarDsigAxis` step `DRIVER_FD_REL * S`, S = max(ft, max|w_a|), so the damaged tangent is the same in MPa, kPa and Pa (kernel check E9, oracle C6).
