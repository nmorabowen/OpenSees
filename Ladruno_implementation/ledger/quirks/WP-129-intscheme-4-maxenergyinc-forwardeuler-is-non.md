### IntScheme 4 (`MaxEnergyInc` -> ForwardEuler) is NON-DETERMINISTIC: it passes uninitialised `nG, nK` into the sub-steps (WP-129, measured)
- **Bites:** `ManzariDafalias::MaxEnergyInc` declares `double nDGamma, nVoidRatio, nG, nK;` and, when the energy increment exceeds its `TolE = 1e-4`, hands `nG, nK` by reference to the sub-step integrator, which reads them before writing them. The plastic results then depend on stack garbage: the same 3D deck run twice in ONE process (unmodified WP-127 binary) gives different committed stresses on every plastic step, and a different answer after another deck has run. The elastic stage is unaffected. Of the other two schemes routed through `MaxEnergyInc`: IntScheme 0 (ModifiedEuler inside it) IS exposed -- ModifiedEuler reads the K, G it is handed (`aC = GetStiffness(K, G)`) before anything writes them; IntScheme 6 (RungeKutta4) is NOT -- it writes K, G (`GetElasticModuli`) before using them. Both were reproducible on the WP-129 decks only because the energy test did not fire with garbage there. Same function, second vanilla defect: its two-half loop advances `cStress, cStrain, cAlpha, cFabric` but never `cEStrain`, so the second half starts from the FIRST half's initial elastic strain and the returned elastic (hence plastic) strain is wrong whenever it sub-steps.
- **Workaround/status:** do not use IntScheme 4 or 0 (bit-for-bit, or the elastic/plastic strain split of 0/4/6 when the energy test fires). WP-129's byte-identity test pins only its elastic-stage rows. Not fixed (vanilla, and it would move every scheme-0/4/6 deck that sub-steps).
- **✅ FIXED — WP-160, [#914](https://github.com/nmorabowen/OpenSees/pull/914) (2026-10-02), with its sibling `MaxStrainInc` (IntScheme 7, 8, 9: same `double nDGamma, nVoidRatio, nG, nK;`, same never-advanced `cEStrain`).** Every sub-step now gets the moduli the un-sub-stepped call gets — the committed G, K, saved at `MaxEnergyInc`'s entry because `RungeKutta4` (IntScheme 6) WRITES the K, G of the full-increment call that precedes the halves — and `cEStrain` advances. Measured on d63f49750 (SRC/material identical to the 117f56060 base): a sub-stepped increment under IntScheme 0, 4, 7, 8 or 9 did not move the stress AT ALL (the garbage read as 0 on that box), the elastic-strain increment was off by 0.6–2.2x (IntScheme 6 too: 0.45), and `ls3d_s4` differed across three `run_all()` passes in one process. After: dsigma = Ce : de_e over the sub-stepped increment to 1–2e-14 (4, 7, 8, 9); halves = two committed steps of their integrator to 4.4e-4 (0) and 6.8e-15 (6); all 18 WP-129 decks bit-identical across three passes, so `NONDETERMINISTIC` is empty and `ls3d_s4` is pinned in full (re-pinned with s6–s9). A cEStrain-only mutant fails all six regression gates. Dispatch deliberately UNCHANGED (owner decision): `MaxStrainInc` still sends 7 (`MAXSTR_MFE`) and 8 (`MAXSTR_RK`) to ForwardEuler. Gate: `tests/test_manzari_substep_moduli.py`. **Test lesson:** a split-vs-sub-stepped comparison for the ForwardEuler schemes is NOT independent of FE's own accuracy on a pre-WP-158 build (each committed FE step lands inside the yield surface and is re-intersected elastically; internal sub-steps never are) — the frozen-moduli identity dsigma = Ce : de_e is. After WP-158 (#901) merged, the split comparison also holds for 4 and 7-9 (5.2e-4 / 1.1e-3 on 5300da720 + WP-160; 1.0 before) and is gated too; the WP-129 decks were re-pinned on that combined build — s4, s7, s8, s9 moved again with the FE fix, s6 (RK4) did not.
