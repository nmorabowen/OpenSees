### `ElasticTimoshenkoBeam3d` missing `"localAxes"` response and `ASDShellQ4`/`ASDShellT3 -pDelta` unfrozen membrane stress after `loadConst`

#### `ElasticTimoshenkoBeam3d` missing `"localAxes"` response (`SRC/element/elasticBeamColumn/ElasticTimoshenkoBeam3d.cpp`)

* **Symptom:** When a horizontal `LadrunoMembrane` slab transferred gravity load onto perimeter `ElasticTimoshenkoBeam3d` beams declared with ETABS local axes (`vecxz = (0, -1, 0)` so local $y = +Z_{\text{global}}$ and local $z = -Y_{\text{global}}$), the beam shear $V_2$ and bending moment $M_3$ had the wrong sign (or dropped into local axis 3) unless the user swapped `vecxz` to `(0, 0, 1)` and negated `f[1]`/`f[5]`. Additionally, `ops.eleResponse(tag, "localAxes")` returned empty `[]`.
* **Root cause:** `LadrunoMembrane::resolveFrameLayout()` queries `frame->setResponse(&argv_la, 1, s)` with `"localAxes"` to obtain the beam's true `CrdTransf` basis `(e_x, e_y, e_z)` before projecting sub-segment line loads `(wx, wy, wz)` into local `(q_x, q_y, q_z)`. Unlike `ElasticBeam3d`, `ElasticTimoshenkoBeam3d::setResponse` and `getResponse` omitted `"localAxes"`, forcing `LadrunoMembrane` into its fallback `_frameDefaultLocalAxes(ex, ey, ez)`, which hardcodes the OpenSees `vecxz = +Z` convention (`ey = (-ex_y, ex_x, 0)`, `ez = +Z`). For an ETABS beam with `vecxz = -Y`, that fallback swapped and negated local axes 2 and 3.
* **Fix (`WP-209`):** Implemented `"localAxes"` (`case 30`) in `ElasticTimoshenkoBeam3d::setResponse` and `ElasticTimoshenkoBeam3d::getResponse`, delegating to `theCoordTransf->getLocalAxes(vx, vy, vz)` and packing the 9-component vector `[vx(0..2), vy(0..2), vz(0..2)]` identically to `ElasticBeam3d`.

#### `ASDShellQ4` / `ASDShellT3 -pDelta` unfrozen membrane stress in $\mathbf{K}_\sigma$ after `loadConst` (`SRC/element/shell/ASDShellQ4.{h,cpp}`, `ASDShellT3.{h,cpp}`, `SRC/domain/domain/Domain.{h,cpp}`)

* **Symptom:** On a wide shear wall meshed with `ASDShellQ4 -pDelta` or `ASDShellT3 -pDelta`, applying gravity followed by `ops.loadConst()` and then either (a) a linear lateral load step (`ops.algorithm("Linear")` or `"Newton"`) or (b) per-mode `ops.responseSpectrumAnalysis(..., "-increment")`:
  1. Under `Linear` static analysis, the horizontal base shear reaction $\sum R_x$ was short of the applied lateral load $F_x$ by $P_{\text{grav}} / (E A_{\text{wall}})$ ($\approx 2.18\%$ on a $4\,\text{m} \times 9\,\text{m}$ wall), and a spurious vertical base reaction appeared.
  2. Under `Newton` static analysis, equilibrium was restored at the cost of shifting the lateral displacement by the same $P_{\text{grav}} / (E A_{\text{wall}})$ relative to standard linearized P-Delta theory ($\mathbf{K}_T = \mathbf{K}_M + \mathbf{K}_\sigma(\mathbf{N}_0)$).
  3. Under `responseSpectrumAnalysis -increment`, the modal reaction increment $\mathbf{R}(\mathbf{u}_0 + \Delta\mathbf{u}_m) - \mathbf{R}(\mathbf{u}_0)$ was contaminated by both a linear cross-term $\mathbf{K}_\sigma(\Delta\mathbf{N}_m)\,\mathbf{u}_0$ ($2.18\%$ base shear error) and a quadratic term $\mathbf{K}_\sigma(\Delta\mathbf{N}_m)\,\Delta\mathbf{u}_m$ (spurious vertical base reaction and asymmetric $+1/-1$ modal reactions).
* **Root cause:** `ASDShellQ4::calculateAll` and `ASDShellT3::calculateAll` always formed $\mathbf{K}_\sigma(\mathbf{N})$ from the **current** trial membrane forces $\mathbf{N}(\mathbf{u}_0 + \Delta\mathbf{u}) = \mathbf{N}_0 + \Delta\mathbf{N}$ and added $\mathbf{K}_\sigma(\mathbf{N}_0 + \Delta\mathbf{N})\,(\mathbf{u}_0 + \Delta\mathbf{u})$ to the internal force vector while omitting $(\partial\mathbf{K}_\sigma/\partial\mathbf{u})\,\mathbf{u}$ from the tangent. Because wide shear walls carry lateral overturning via vertical tension/compression membrane stress increments $\Delta n_{22} = \pm M y / I$, re-evaluating $\mathbf{K}_\sigma(\mathbf{N}_0 + \Delta\mathbf{N})$ during post-`loadConst` perturbations introduced $\mathbf{K}_\sigma(\Delta\mathbf{N})\,\mathbf{u}_0 + \mathbf{K}_\sigma(\Delta\mathbf{N})\,\Delta\mathbf{u}$ into the resisting force even though the predictor tangent solved with $\mathbf{K}_\sigma(\mathbf{N}_0)$.
* **Fix (`WP-209`):** Added `Domain::isLoadConstant()` (`m_loadConstActive` set in `Domain::setLoadConstant()` and cleared in `unsetLoadConstant()`, `revertToStart()`, and `clearAll()`). In `ASDShellQ4` and `ASDShellT3`, `commitState()` snapshots the committed local membrane forces `(n11, n22, n12)` at each Gauss point into `m_pdelta_N` whenever `m_pdelta` is active and `!getDomain()->isLoadConstant()`. Once `isLoadConstant()` is true, `calculateAll` evaluates $\mathbf{K}_\sigma$ from the frozen preload stress `m_pdelta_N` so that $\mathbf{K}_T = \mathbf{K}_M + \mathbf{K}_\sigma(\mathbf{N}_0)$ is constant and equals the exact derivative of $\mathbf{R}_{\text{int}}(\mathbf{u}_0 + \Delta\mathbf{u})$.
