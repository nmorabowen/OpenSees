### An ADR's early "options considered" section can be SUPERSEDED by its own §12 implementation log -- so grepping one section and concluding "X is missing" produces a confident, wrong finding
- **Bites:** any agent or human surveying what the fork does and does not have by keyword-grepping an ADR. Discovered building [[86_geomechanics_libraries_and_mpm_scoping_report]]: ADR-71 §6 ("Explicit dynamics: the honest note") lists three routes for staggered/explicit u-p -- Zienkiewicz-Paul-Chan 1988, Huang-Zienkiewicz fractional step, Xu et al. 2021 -- and does NOT name the fixed-stress split. Grepping §6 alone yielded the conclusion "fixed-stress is a genuine hole in the option set and the cheapest thing to close", which went into a report's executive summary as its flagship recommendation. It is wrong.
- **Why:** ADR-71 §6 was written at draft time and superseded by ADR-71 **§12** in the SAME FILE (2026-07-13 entry: "The staggered architecture was then tried end-to-end and works: fixed-stress split ... 3 iters vs 11"; and "**P7 OPENED as [[73_ladruno_porous_overlay_adr]]** ... fixed-stress staggered overlay carrying the §6 staggered route"). ADR-73 then SHIPPED it and measured it: "Coupling = fixed-stress staggered split, solid-first" (:112), "≡ monolithic backward Euler: crack probe 0.00 %, Terzaghi 2.8e-8; both sub-solves SPD; factorization ×3.3 cheaper" (:67), with Kim-Tchelepi-Juanes 2011, Mikelic-Wheeler 2013 (the contraction proof) and Turska-Schrefler 1993 all already in its reference list. This fork's ADRs are LIVING documents whose §12 logs routinely overturn their own drafted §§1-9; the drafted text is the plan, the log is the state.
- **Workaround/status (2026-08-23, ADR-86 adversarial review -- caught by a Fable review before the plan was acted on, not by the author):** any claim of the form "the fork lacks X" MUST cite (a) the ADR's §12 implementation log, and (b) the companion ADRs named in its header, before it is written down. A keyword grep over §§1-9 is evidence of what was *planned*, never of what exists. Note the phase-numbering corollary: ADR-71's phase table still lists **P7 as "(research)"**, which is likewise stale -- P7 was opened and carried by ADR-73. The report's §8 carries the generalised rule; §11 carries the full finding.
