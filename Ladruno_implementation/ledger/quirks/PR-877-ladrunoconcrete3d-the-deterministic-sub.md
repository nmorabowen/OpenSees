### LadrunoConcrete3D: the deterministic sub-incrementation is DISCONTINUOUS (n boundaries, ladder switches) and its tangent is the CHAIN's, not the last piece's (WP concrete3d-hang-diagnosis, [#877](https://github.com/nmorabowen/OpenSees/pull/877))
- **Bites:** `-subIncr deterministic` (the default) integrates every plastic trial in `n = ceil(f_tr/0.3)` pieces. A chain of `n` and one of `n + 1` pieces (or two ladder levels) are two different, each consistent, integrations of the same increment: `sigma_eff` jumps 0.06-0.40 MPa on 15-41 MPa (0.4-1.5 %) at `n` boundaries, and in tension near first cracking the ladder switched DENSELY until r4 (review 3 of #877, MAJOR 1; fixed, see status): the direct tension return failed intermittently once kappa_p passes about 2, so the serving level is a coin flip on rounding (1691-2212 level flips in 4000 points at 1e-9 relative spacing, effective-stress jumps up to 0.32 MPa, 30-97 % direct-return failures in 2e-6-wide windows). Consequences: nominally identical Gauss points differ (hosting parity ~1e-3, EAS alpha ~1e-11), `F` vs `Q@F` objectivity holds only on one branch, and a finite-difference tangent reference that lets a leg straddle a boundary differentiates the jump.
- **The tangent:** the chain is differentiated forward through the pieces with the kappa-history coupling (`PieceSens`); ignoring that coupling left 6e-2..7e-1 error at n = 2..64. Pin `n` on both FD legs (`Params::subIncrForceN`, `returnMapPieces()`, oracle `subincr_force_n`).
- **Workaround/status:** documented, tests derive tolerances from the measured jump. **The dense ladder switching is fixed** (r4 rescue of #877, 2026-10-30, ADR-31 section 17): its root cause was the direct tension return, whose plain Newton fails once kappa_p passes ~2 and whose `newtonGlob` rescue walks out of the admissible set; in tension `returnMapHardening` now uses a fixed root order (the vertex, then a bracketed nested solve keeping the least-hardening root, accepted on the full residual; the plain Newton and `newtonGlob` only if it finds no bracket; compression keeps the PR-head order). Measured: 0 direct-return failures in the review windows (were 645-1953 of 2000), 0 level flips in the fine and coarse scans (were 1679-2203 / 8257). The `n` boundaries remain (63 in the coarse scan) and so does their jump; `refuse` stays opt-in for now: re-measured on the fixed kernel (37a353fff) it reaches B1 11.6 mm (deterministic 10.0, both at the wall time) and C3 26.2 mm (deterministic 31.4), against 1.69 / 14.4 mm before (ADR-31 section 17). `substeps` counts forced chains too.
#### Follow-up (ADR-31, apeGmsh #2621 F2 port)
- The ladder's chain was already right; the two other sub-stepped paths were not. `-subIncr adaptive` and the deterministic mode's adaptive RESCUE (ladder exhausted) reported the last piece's one-piece tangent (0.26-0.55 p50 off the chain over their own schedule on the corbel record dumps), and a tension-dead point whose compressive remainder took n >= 2 pieces read d/d(deps) C^-1 of the zero-increment chain (2.85 off its FD). Both now report the chain (`chainPieceSens`, `wrtStart`); kernel check E1, D10, D11. See `ADR-31-ladrunoconcrete3d-f2-port-damaged-tangent.md`.
