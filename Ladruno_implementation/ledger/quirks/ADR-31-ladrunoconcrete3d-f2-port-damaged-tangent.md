### LadrunoConcrete3D: the damaged tangent was wrong at the omega cap, at the tension apex, in SI units, under -eta, and on the adaptive, rescue and tension-dead-cut paths (apeGmsh #2621 F2 port; supersedes #1050)
- **Bites (6b7ebf3a8, kernel check D-tests and the corbel record dumps):** the reported `Dtan6` was not the derivative of the map the material integrates, in seven places. (1) A bilinear omega_t, or a CDPM2-drive omega_c, exactly AT `OMEGA_MAX` fell through to the exponential-law IFT: 27 off its FD (D6a). It accounts for nearly all of the 15.8-22.3 % of corbel states whose damaged tangent was more than 1e-3 off, and almost every refused sigma_22 loop passes through it: D22 p90 52.7 off its FD there. (2) At the tension apex the per-component micro-FD of the damage drivers averaged two meridian slopes: 1.00 (D7). (3) `dscalarDsig` stepped an absolute 1e-6 stress units (#2639): 7.5e-5 / 6.6e-2 / 81 in MPa / kPa / Pa on a far-compressive path (D9). (4) Under `-eta` the beta_c, ctTemper-proj and tcTemper-proj micro-FDs ran the INVISCID return map: 3.3e-3 (0.10 in Pa, D8) and 2.7e-3 for the proj weights alone (D8b). (5) `-subIncr adaptive` and (6) the deterministic mode's adaptive rescue reported the last piece's tangent: 0.26-0.55 p50 off the chain over their own schedule on the corbel dumps (E1, D10). (7) A tension-dead point whose compressive remainder took n >= 2 pieces read d/d(deps) C^-1 of a zero-increment chain: 2.85 (D11). `damagedTangent` also ran up to 25 return maps per call (E4).
- **Now:** `damagedTangent` is closed form and runs no return map (`returnMapTensor` is declared after it; `KernelCallCounters`). The cap is flat, the apex gradient is taken along the axis, both driver steps are 1e-6 S with S = max(ft, max|sig_bar_a|), the kappa row `dkpDeps` comes out of every return path and through the Duvaut-Lions blend (`dkp *= beta`) and the cut, the adaptive and rescue paths report `chainPieceSens` over their pieces, and the cut differentiates with respect to its start (`wrtStart`). All of D6-D11 and E1-E5 pass in MPa, kPa and Pa under UCRT and libmmt; 36 / 37 of them fail on 6b7ebf3a8.
- **Stress and state at the 3-D material point are bit-exact** against 6b7ebf3a8: all 42 398 corbel records (92 858 evaluations, R-record passes included) and 86 400 driven steps (3 sub-incrementation modes), in MPa, kPa and Pa, UCRT and libmmt. **The views that read the tangent move:** the confined fibre on 100 of 21 600 steps (deterministic, legacy flow, hoopK 0; at most 6.6e-10 of max(|sigma|, ft)), refusals unchanged. The plane-stress sigma_22 Newton moves on every mode, but its refusals move only on the legacy flow (deterministic 282-300 -> 325-374, adaptive and refuse 1057-1094 -> 990-1027, depending on the unit system and libm). The CDPM2 flow, the default and the corbel's, has 0 refusals before and after, and its converged stresses agree to 1.3e-8. On the legacy flow, before the first refusal change, converged stresses differ by up to 1.3e-3 (deterministic) and, in one kPa UCRT drive, by 1.0 where the two Newtons converge to different roots. The F1 replay fixture moves accordingly (its commit message lists every moved line by cause).
- **Workaround/status:** fixed. What is still open, listed and not waived: (a) the effective tangent on driven states with two equal principal stresses (the drives with equal lateral strains) is 1e-6 to 1e-4 off its FD on 6b7ebf3a8 and here alike, so the driven-path damaged p90 stays effective-limited (1.2-3.6e-6). (b) Most rescue states have no two-sided FD (a schedule piece converges at the centre only): they are checked against the chain over their own schedule (0 difference), and the few measurable ones read up to 1e-5 one-sided. (c) On a point with two equal tensile principal stresses, the ctTemper-proj weight depends on the eigenbasis chosen, so the nominal stress is discontinuous at round-off: 2 driven states. This is a state-path defect. (d) The Eq. 57 rho guard and the 1e-8 eigen gap are absolute (#2645). (e) The corbel now runs in 23 / 36 s instead of 615 / 630 s and refuses at 0.4297 / 0.4281 mm instead of 0.4375 mm. The remaining refusals are sigma_22 loops that cycle or run away with an exact D22 (p90 6e-9): sigma_22(eps_22) changes slope sign inside the loop on 187 of 253 loops, so this calls for a globalized sigma_22 solve, not a tangent fix.
