### LadrunoConcrete3D: the damaged tangent was wrong at the omega cap, at the tension apex, in SI units, under -eta, on the adaptive, rescue and tension-dead-cut paths, and in the kappa row on the compressive meridian (apeGmsh #2621 F2 port; supersedes #1050)
- **Bites (6b7ebf3a8, kernel check D-tests and the corbel record dumps):** the reported `Dtan6` was not the derivative of the map the material integrates, in eight places. (1) A bilinear omega_t, or a CDPM2-drive omega_c, exactly AT `OMEGA_MAX` fell through to the exponential-law IFT: 27 off its FD (D6a). It accounts for nearly all of the 15.8-22.3 % of corbel states whose damaged tangent was more than 1e-3 off, and almost every refused sigma_22 loop passes through it: D22 p90 52.7 off its FD there. (2) At the tension apex the per-component micro-FD of the damage drivers averaged two meridian slopes: 1.00 (D7). (3) `dscalarDsig` stepped an absolute 1e-6 stress units (#2639): 7.5e-5 / 6.6e-2 / 81 in MPa / kPa / Pa on a far-compressive path (D9). (4) Under `-eta` the beta_c, ctTemper-proj and tcTemper-proj micro-FDs ran the INVISCID return map: 3.3e-3 (0.10 in Pa, D8) and 2.7e-3 for the proj weights alone (D8b). (5) `-subIncr adaptive` and (6) the deterministic mode's adaptive rescue reported the last piece's tangent: 0.26-0.55 p50 off the chain over their own schedule on the corbel dumps (E1, D10). (7) A tension-dead point whose compressive remainder took n >= 2 pieces read d/d(deps) C^-1 of a zero-increment chain: 2.85 (D11). (8) On the compressive meridian (two equal principal trial stresses: every uniaxial and axisymmetric compression state) `principalJacobian` took d cos2/dw, the derivative of R4's Lode factor, by a central micro-FD; theta = acos(cos 3theta)/3 has a square-root kink there, and the micro-FD read the round-off in cos 3theta as a slope (2.8e-4 for an exact 0), so the kappa row was 1.6e-3 off on a uniaxial direct return (0.7e-2 to 1.8e-2 against an exact reference, D12) and the damaged tangent 3e-5 off at the omega_c onset step. `damagedTangent` also ran up to 25 return maps per call (E4).
- **Now:** `damagedTangent` is closed form and runs no return map (`returnMapTensor` is declared after it; `KernelCallCounters`). The cap is flat, the apex gradient is taken along the axis, both driver steps are 1e-6 S with S = max(ft, max|sig_bar_a|), the kappa row `dkpDeps` comes out of every return path and through the Duvaut-Lions blend (`dkp *= beta`) and the cut, the adaptive and rescue paths report `chainPieceSens` over their pieces, the cut differentiates with respect to its start (`wrtStart`), and `principalJacobian` takes d cos2/dw in closed form (the regular map's twin of `vertexPrincipalJacobian`'s dc/dw, same guard; with lodeFactor 1 including the axis-weight term). All of D6-D13 (with D12b and D13r) and E1-E5 pass in MPa, kPa and Pa under UCRT and libmmt; D12, D12b and D13 fail on 63f5f1393's kernel (D13r passes there, at 1.6e-8 to 5.0e-8 across the units and libms: it pins the rescue's chain, which that kernel already had inside its 1e-7 bound), and 36 / 37 of the earlier checks on 6b7ebf3a8. D12, D12b, D13 and D13r compare against EXACT references (no FD of the map): the IFT of `cdpm2HardeningResidual` with an exact (meridian), residual-FD (off-meridian) or analytic vertex right-hand side, an independent spectral assembly, chained over the pieces. They are exact given the residual's analytic Jacobian, and they share its helpers (`cdpm2FlowGradJac`, `dDuctilityXhdSigV`) with the kernel: a mutant in either helper passes all four and is caught by E1, E1d, D11, the chain check and the `tanf` oracle records, whose FD runs none of them.
- **Stress and state at the 3-D material point are bit-exact** against 6b7ebf3a8: all 42 398 corbel records (92 858 evaluations, R-record passes included) and 86 400 driven steps (3 sub-incrementation modes), in MPa, kPa and Pa, UCRT and libmmt. **The views that read the tangent move:** the confined fibre on 100 of 21 600 steps (deterministic, legacy flow, hoopK 0; at most 6.6e-10 of max(|sigma|, ft)), refusals unchanged. The plane-stress sigma_22 Newton moves on every mode, but its refusals move only on the legacy flow (deterministic 282-300 -> 325-374, adaptive and refuse 1057-1094 -> 990-1027, depending on the unit system and libm). The CDPM2 flow, the default and the corbel's, has 0 refusals before and after, and its converged stresses agree to 1.3e-8. On the legacy flow, before the first refusal change, converged stresses differ by up to 1.3e-3 (deterministic) and, in one kPa UCRT drive, by 1.0 where the two Newtons converge to different roots. The F1 replay fixture moves accordingly (its commit message lists every moved line by cause).
- **Workaround/status:** fixed. Settled against exact references: (a) the strata where the FD of the map read over its bound after the fix are FD limits, not tangent errors. The driven-path effective p90 (4.6e-7 to 5.6e-7) and kink +side (up to 2.1e-5) sit exactly on a meridian or at the tension vertex, where the map itself carries a ~sqrt(eps) theta round-off (and the vertex root is exact only to its bisection bracket); the kernel matches the exact reference there to <= 3.6e-11 (effective) and <= 2.5e-10 (damaged), and to <= 1.6e-10 on all 27 330 live driven states. The corbel's R >= 5 pieces kink -side state (1.08e-6 to 2.13e-6, one state) matches to 8.3e-12 (effective) and 3.0e-8 to 5.3e-8 (damaged, converged in h). (b) Rescue states: the kernel matches the exact chain over each state's own adaptive schedule to <= 1.65e-11 on every corbel rescue state and <= 7.7e-10 on D13r's; 2 cap-entry states have no derivative (the nominal stress changes branch at round-off). D13r pins the rescue's `Dtan6` and kappa row; the damaged tangent on rescue states, those 2 included, rests on the corbel probes and no kernel check pins it. Still open, listed and not waived: (c) on a tension-dead point with two equal tensile principal stresses the ctTemper-proj weight depends on the eigenbasis chosen, so the nominal stress is discontinuous at round-off (2 driven states, seq 2404072 / 2504072, damaged error 3.5e-3; earlier the same class): a state-path defect, its own slice (nmorabowen/apeGmsh#2662). Under Duvaut-Lions the damaged map has no 6x6 derivative at the tension apex: on D13's 110 apex states per unit system the one-sided derivatives along e_0 differ by at least 0.86 of the right one. The derivative along the hydrostatic direction does exist there, and D13 holds `D e_h` to the FD of the map to 1e-6. (d) The Eq. 57 rho guard and the 1e-8 eigen gap are absolute (#2645). (e) The corbel still refuses, and its response is chaotic, so a single run does not characterise a kernel: on one pyd of this kernel (MKL_CBWR unset), ft x (1 + 1e-12 / 2e-12 / 3e-12) reads peak 170.6 / 349.3 / 318.1 kN per side, delta 0.15 / 0.4875 / 0.4625 mm and 32 / 194 / 154 s. The office PC's runs are therefore single samples, not kernel properties, and comparing them shows no effect of a kernel on reach or cost. Unset / COMPATIBLE, on this kernel: 29 / 23 points, delta 0.4362 / 0.4344 mm, 189 / 136 s, 875 / 723 refusals; on 63f5f1393: 18 / 21 points, 0.4297 / 0.4281 mm, 23-24 / 36-37 s, 253 / 350 refusals; on ladruno 6b7ebf3a8: 26 / 24 points, 0.4375 mm, 615 / 630 s. The sampled refusals are sigma_22-condensation failures (D22 changes sign in the loop, run-aways). On this kernel's two samples F3's phase B accepts 82 % / 85 % of them; the rest are piece-count jumps (F2b) and failed returns. Any claim about reach, cost or refusal count needs an ensemble.
