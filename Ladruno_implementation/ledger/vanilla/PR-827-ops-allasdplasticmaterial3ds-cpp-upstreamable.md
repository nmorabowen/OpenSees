| `SRC/material/nD/ASDPlasticMaterial3D/YieldFunctions/StiffSoilShear_YF.h` | `// Ladruno (ADR-97 P5)`: `qf` was `(c*cot(phi)+sigma3)*2*sin(phi)/(1-sin(phi))`, and `cot(phi) -> Inf` at `phi == 0` (a legitimate cohesive-only/undrained-clay choice); `Inf * 2*sin(phi)` (`== Inf*0` at `phi==0`) is the IEEE-754 indeterminate NaN.
