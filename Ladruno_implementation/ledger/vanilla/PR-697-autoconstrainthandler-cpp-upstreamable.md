| `SRC/analysis/handler/AutoConstraintHandler.cpp`, `SRC/analysis/handler/AutoConstraintHandler.h` | `// Ladruno (ADR-80 S2)`: **`constraints Auto` + a non-homogeneous `sp` was a SILENT WRONG ANSWER.** `AutoConstraintHandler::applyLoad()` ran the two `enforceSPs` loops but omitted the `updateElement()` loop that `TransformationConstraintHandler::enforceSPs` has at `:518-521`. Both handlers route SP-constrained nodes through a `TransformationDOF_Group`, so the DOF is genuinely eliminated (`setID(dof,-1)`) and there is no `K*du_prescribed` column — the **only** way the prescribed motion reaches the RHS is through the state of the elements attached to the driven node. Without the loop the first residual of every step is stale, `dU ~ 0`, and a dU-based convergence test declares victory at iteration 1 (`CTestNormDispIncr` has no minimum-iteration guard, `:160-175`); the step commits with **only the driven layer moved** and `analyze()` returns 0. Measured A/B on two equal trusses driven to 3.0 (ADR-80 gate G4, pre-fix binary): `Transformation` u_mid = **1.5 in 2 iterations**, `Auto` u_mid = **0.0 in 1 iteration**; identical signature on two stacked `stdBrick`s. **Test-dependence is the mechanism** and is why it hid: `NormDispIncr` *and* `EnergyIncr` both commit the wrong answer, while `NormUnbalance` sees the large stale residual, refuses to converge at iteration 1, and the next `LoadControl::update` → `updateDomain()` silently repairs it — the same model is right or wrong purely by choice of test. (The ADR-80 findings note predicted `CTestNormDispIncr` only; `EnergyIncr` is an additional casualty found at G4.) Fix mirrors the Transformation handler: a `theFEs` member holding the FE_Elements that touch an SP-constrained node (membership computed in `handle()` from the existing `sp_map`, O(1) per node), the `updateElement()` loop in `applyLoad()`, and a `clearAll()` reset. Answer-changing **by design** for the broken case; homogeneous-only (`fix`-only) models are numerically unaffected — `enforceSPs` rewrites an unchanged value, so the added update is idempotent. Gated by `tests/test_auto_handler_sp_update.py` (verified to FAIL on the pre-fix binary). **Fork-only (decided 2026-08-04) — deliberately NOT offered upstream**; it would apply cleanly there, but upstreaming a behaviour-changing handler fix is its own workstream and was not opened. | [#697](https://github.com/nmorabowen/OpenSees/pull/697) |
